About

I’m an undergraduate at UNC–Chapel Hill double majoring in Computer Science and Computational Physics, with a minor in Screenwriting.

I like building things at the intersection of machine learning, data engineering, and storytelling. A lot of my recent work has focused on how large language models reason, how to combine many model samples into better decisions, and how to make technical ideas feel intuitive and fun.

When I’m not poking at GPUs, you’ll probably find me teaching, writing comedy, or hanging out with animals.
